# People and shared threads

Each paired device belongs to a person, Luke or Pauli. Every new thread belongs to the
person whose device started it, and threads that agents start belong to the owner of
the thread that started them. Threads from before people existed, and threads from
unlabelled devices such as the host's own desktop app, belong to Luke.

This is a view, not access control. Every paired device can still open, read and
write every thread.

## Label a device

On the host, open **Settings → Connections** and choose the person in the device's
row. The label applies to that device's new threads from then on. A device picks up
its new label the next time its window comes back into focus, or when it reloads.

## Pick whose threads to show

On web or desktop, choose **Luke**, **Pauli**, **Shared** or **All** in the picker at
the top of the sidebar. It filters the standard and Promachos sidebars.

- **Luke** or **Pauli** shows the threads that person owns or shares.
- **Shared** shows only shared threads.
- **All** shows every thread.

The picker starts on this device's person, and each browser or desktop app remembers
its own choice. Unsent drafts count as this device's person.

## Share a thread

In a thread's chat header, the owner opens the sharing menu and chooses **Share with
Pauli** (or **Share with Luke**). The thread gets a **Shared** label and shows under
both people and under **Shared**.

- The owner chooses **Unshare** to remove the other person and the label.
- The other person chooses **Leave** to remove only themselves. The owner keeps the
  thread.

Each change appears as a line in the thread.
